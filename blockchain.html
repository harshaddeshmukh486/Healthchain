🔐 How Hash Chaining Works

Every report gets a unique SHA-256 cryptographic hash computed securely in your browser.

Each new block stores the previous block's hash — creating an unbreakable cryptographic chain.

Any malicious change in a record alters its hash completely, breaking the continuity.

We verify your entire chain every time you open this page to instantly detect tampering.
